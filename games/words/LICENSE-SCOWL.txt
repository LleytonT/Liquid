The five-letter lists in lists.json are derived from final/english-words.10, .20, .35, .40, .50, .55, .60, .70, and .80. Words in misc/offensive.* and misc/profane.* were removed. Answer bands follow those SCOWL size classes: common = size 10, familiar = size 20, wider = size 35. The valid-guess list is every remaining five-letter word from those files.
